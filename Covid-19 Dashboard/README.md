🦠 COVID-19 Dashboard:
I am excited to showcase my Excel Data Analytics project: a COVID-19 Dashboard. This dashboard is a demonstration of my ability to build interactive data visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs) from complex global datasets. The analysis successfully transforms raw data on cases, deaths, recoveries, testing, and vaccination into actionable, time-based insights for strategic evaluation of the pandemic's impact.

🔴 Key Insights:
The dashboard presents the following total cumulative global figures:
Total Cases: 498,531,362

Total Deaths: 24,861,853

Total Recovered: 448,454,214

Total Vaccination Doses: 1,005,787,20
 
💫 Highlights:
High Recovery Rate: Globally, recovery has been highly successful. With ∼448 million recovered cases out of ∼498 million total cases, the approximate global recovery rate is very high (∼90%).
High-Burden Nations: The USA and Germany have the highest cumulative case counts among the sample (each over ∼5.0 million) and have also administered the highest number of vaccine doses, suggesting significant past disease spread and robust mitigation efforts.
Regional Hotspots: Within the sampled states/regions, Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo stand out with the highest total cases (∼30 million and ∼18 million, respectively), indicating these Brazilian states were significant epicenters.
Vaccination Leaders: The top five countries (USA, Germany, India, Brazil, UK) have all administered doses in the ∼195 to ∼210 million range, showing a similar large-scale vaccine distribution effort among major nations.
Seasonal/Wave Pattern: The Monthly New Cases chart shows a significant dip in new cases during the summer months (May to September), followed by a noticeable rise in October, suggesting a recurrent seasonal pattern or the onset of new infection waves during colder periods.
