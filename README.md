🚀 RealMart Sales Dashboard – Data-Driven Insights for Better Decision Making 📊
I’m excited to share my latest Excel-based Data Scientist project, where I designed and developed a RealMart Sales Dashboard to provide clear insights into overall business performance.
The dashboard's design emphasizes Total Sales, Profits, Orders, and Ratings; by providing interactive data visualisations, it facilitates superior decision making.

🔍 Project Insights:

💰 Total Sales: ₹659272

💸 Total Profit: ₹31,394

🛒 Total Orders: 2064

⭐ Average Rating: 7.20

📊 Key insights:
 A full-spectrum analysis was conducted on the sales data, covering all key operational and financial metrics. This included a breakdown of core KPIs (Sales, Profit, Orders, Ratings), a geographic assessment of city and branch revenue, an exploration of time-based sales trends, a comparison of product line sales versus profit, and an investigation into customer behavior through payment method and gender demographics. The use of combo charts provided a direct comparison of Sales vs. Gross Income to establish margin insights.


⚙ Tools & Techniques Used:
 Microsoft Excel | Pivot Tables | Pivot Charts | Slicers | KPI Design | Dashboard Layout | Data Visualization | Analytical Thinking

 This project significantly upgraded my proficiency in business intelligence (BI). I can now effectively analyze complex data, visualize key performance metrics, and engineer interactive dashboards that translate raw data into actionable, decision-support insights.
